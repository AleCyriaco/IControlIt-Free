## Description

Tired of digging through iOS Settings every time you need to turn off Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or GPS? QuickToggle is your personal control panel - access everything in one tap!

**QUICK CONTROL**
- Access Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Location instantly
- Direct shortcuts to 10+ iOS Settings sections
- Real-time status indicators for each service
- Built-in battery monitoring

**SMART PROFILES**
- Pre-configured profiles: Airplane Plus, Battery Saver, Privacy
- Create custom profiles (e.g., Work, Home, Gym)
- Apply settings with a single tap
- Customizable icons for each profile

**AUTOMATIC SCHEDULING**
- Schedule when to turn services on/off
- Quick presets: GPS at night, Wi-Fi at bedtime, Bluetooth at work
- Choose weekdays for repetition
- Smart notifications at scheduled times

**BATTERY SAVINGS**
- See how much battery each service uses
- Estimated savings when turning off services
- Configurable low battery alerts
- Personalized saving tips

**SIRI & SHORTCUTS**
- Voice commands: "Hey Siri, turn off Wi-Fi"
- Shortcuts app integration
- Interactive home screen widget
- URL Schemes for advanced automations

**COMPLETE HISTORY**
- Log of all changes made
- Filter by service, date, or source
- Know exactly when and how each change was made

**MORE SHORTCUTS**
- Clear Safari history
- Battery Health
- Cellular Data
- Focus / Do Not Disturb
- Notifications
- Display & Brightness
- Sounds & Haptics
- VPN
- Storage
- Passwords

Privacy first: QuickToggle doesn't collect any personal data. Everything works locally on your device.

Compatible with iOS 17 and later. Optimized for iPhone and iPad.
