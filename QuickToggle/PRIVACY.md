# Privacy Policy - QuickToggle

**Last updated: February 14, 2026**

## Overview

QuickToggle is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.** Specifically:

- No personal information is collected
- No usage analytics or tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No network requests to external servers

## Data Storage

All app data (profiles, schedules, history, preferences) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is included in your iCloud backup (if enabled)
- Is completely removed when you delete the app

## Permissions

The app may request the following permissions, solely for functionality:

- **Bluetooth**: To detect if Bluetooth is enabled or disabled (read-only status check)
- **Location**: To detect if Location Services are enabled or disabled (read-only status check)
- **Notifications**: To send scheduled reminders for toggle actions you configured

These permissions are optional. The app works without them, but some status indicators may not be available.

## Third-Party Services

QuickToggle does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## Children's Privacy

QuickToggle does not collect any data from any users, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this privacy policy changes, we will update it in this repository and in the App Store listing.
